Renovation costs in Cyprus have risen steadily since 2022, driven by material imports and skilled-labour demand. This guide gives you realistic 2025 figures based on projects we've completed in Limassol.

## Average Cost Per Square Metre

| Specification | Cost per m² |
|---|---|
| Basic / economy | €250–€380 |
| Standard | €380–€600 |
| Premium | €600–€1,000+ |

These figures cover all labour and standard finishes. Custom joinery, imported tiles, or smart-home systems are priced separately.

## What's Included in a Full Renovation?

A complete apartment renovation typically covers:

- **Demolition & disposal** — removing old finishes, tiles, partitions
- **Electrical rewire** — new consumer unit, sockets, lighting circuits
- **Plumbing upgrade** — new supply pipes, waste pipes, heating (if applicable)
- **Plastering & levelling** — walls and ceilings to a smooth finish
- **Tiling** — bathrooms, kitchen, and any tiled areas
- **Flooring** — parquet, laminate, or SPC throughout
- **Painting** — two coats throughout with primer
- **Doors & trims** — internal doors, skirting, and architraves
- **Kitchen installation** — if supplied by client or us

## Hidden Costs to Budget For

Many renovation quotes leave these out:

- **Permits** — structural changes may require a building permit from the Municipality of Limassol (€300–€1,500)
- **Asbestos survey** — mandatory for pre-1990 buildings before demolition
- **Furniture storage** — if you're relocating during renovation
- **Contingency (10–15%)** — for surprises behind walls (corroded pipes, poor previous work)

## How to Get an Accurate Quote

Avoid any contractor who quotes per m² without visiting your property. Every apartment is different — pipe routes, existing conditions, and structural quirks all affect the price.
