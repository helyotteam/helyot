A bathroom renovation is one of the most impactful upgrades you can make to your home. In Limassol's competitive property market, a well-finished bathroom adds significant value and transforms daily living. Here's what to expect when you renovate with our team.

## How Long Does a Bathroom Renovation Take?

A standard bathroom (4–8 m²) typically takes **10–15 working days** from demo to handover. Larger bathrooms with custom features can take 3–4 weeks.

The timeline breaks down roughly as:

- **Demo & waterproofing** — 2 days
- **Plumbing & electrical rough-in** — 2–3 days
- **Tiling** — 3–5 days
- **Fixtures & finishing** — 2–3 days
- **Snagging & handover** — 1 day

## What Does Bathroom Renovation Cost in Limassol?

Costs vary by specification:

- **Economy fit-out** — €3,000–€5,000
- **Standard renovation** — €5,000–€9,000
- **Premium / designer** — €9,000–€18,000+

Prices include labour, waterproofing, tiling, fixtures, and waste removal. Materials are excluded unless you opt for a turnkey package.

## Choosing the Right Tiles

Large-format tiles (60×120 cm or 80×160 cm) are popular in Limassol for their clean, seamless look. Rectified porcelain is the most practical choice — durable, easy to clean, and resistant to the island's humidity.

For floors, choose tiles with an **R10 or higher slip-resistance rating**, especially in wet zones.

## Waterproofing Is Not Optional

Cyprus humidity makes thorough waterproofing essential. We apply a liquid membrane system to all walls and floors in the wet zone, following EN 14891 standards. This is a hidden cost that some contractors skip — don't let them.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Skipping the structural survey** — old apartments may have corroded pipes hidden in walls.
2. **Choosing tiles before finalising layout** — cuts waste significantly.
3. **Underestimating ventilation** — Cyprus summers make a powerful extractor fan essential.
4. **DIY plumbing** — Cyprus law requires licensed plumbers for any supply-line work.
