Germasogeia is the busiest renovation market in Limassol, and for a simple reason: two things line up here. A large stock of 1980–2000s housing that has reached the end of its patience, and the densest short-let market on the coast.

What it costs, what it returns, and what is specific to this district.

## In short

A two-bedroom flat in Germasogeia costs **€25,000–33,600** at standard specification, up to €44,000–56,000 at premium. A 250 m² villa runs **€70,000–225,000** depending on specification.

A renovated two-bed near the sea earns **€130–190 a night** in high season against €70–95 unrenovated, at 75–90% occupancy instead of 55–70%. The difference pays the work back in roughly **three to four seasons**.

The local catch is the tourist-boom stock of the 1980s. Full rewiring and new windows are near-certain, and that belongs in the sums before you buy, not after.

## What the housing is

### Blocks from 1980–2000

The bulk of the district: three to eight storeys built through the tourist boom. What is close to guaranteed:

| Item | Why | Cost on 85 m² |
|---|---|---|
| Full rewire | aluminium or tired copper, no earthing | €2,800–4,500 |
| New windows | single glazing, no thermal break | €4,000–9,000 |
| Tanking to bathrooms | done off-standard or not at all | €600–1,200 |
| Replacing risers within the flat | corrosion, blockages | €800–1,500 |

Windows deserve their own note. Original single-glazed aluminium without a thermal break gives you heat loss, winter condensation and road noise from the Tourist Road all at once. Replacement almost always lands in the quote.

### Newer developments, 2010s onward

Better build quality, services still sound. Here renovation is not restoration but raising the class for letting: joinery, lighting, wet-area finishes.

Budget is usually **€300–410 per m²** — standard specification without the first-fix work.

### Villas and townhouses

Concentrated on the hillside above the Tourist Road, which brings its own item: a change in level means retaining walls, drainage and terracing. That is a separate budget line flat plots never carry — covered in [the piece on retaining walls](/en/blog/retaining-walls-cyprus).

## What it returns

A two-bedroom within walking distance of the sea.

| | Unrenovated | After a standard renovation |
|---|---|---|
| Long let | €900–1,200/month | €1,400–1,800/month |
| High season, nightly | €70–95 | €130–190 |
| Season occupancy | 55–70% | 75–90% |
| High-season income | €7,000–11,000 | €18,000–25,000 |

That is **€9,000–14,000** a season of difference. Against €30,000 invested it is three to four seasons, then clean upside plus a higher sale price.

## What costs money here specifically

**Management companies.** Almost every block in Germasogeia has one, with its own rules: working hours, goods-lift access, consent for anything touching the facade. An air-conditioning unit fixed to the elevation without consent comes off at your expense. Check the rules before the quote, not after.

**Salt.** Much of the district sits within a kilometre of the sea. Ironmongery, hinges, runners and any external metal corrode inside 2–4 years unless they are stainless or powder-coated. The purchase difference is 10–15%; the service-life difference is threefold.

**Seasonality.** From June to September, blocks living on short lets often restrict noisy work. The real window for a full cycle is October to May.

**Access.** Narrow streets and dense building. Waste removal and material deliveries get harder, adding 10–20% to strip-out on some addresses.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can work be done in summer?**
Technically yes, but blocks with short lets often restrict noisy work. Plan a full cycle for October to May or expect the programme to stretch.

**Is management company consent needed?**
For work inside the flat, usually not. For anything touching the facade, roof, risers or common property, yes — and get it in writing.

**What do new windows cost in an older block?**
€4,000–9,000 on an 85 m² flat depending on the number of openings and the profile. On elevations facing the Tourist Road, acoustic glazing is worth the difference.

**Is a 1980s flat worth buying to renovate?**
Often yes — the entry price is lower and after a full cycle the property catches the newer blocks on rental rate. But budget for a full rewire and new windows: €7,000–13,000 above a normal quote.

**Do you work on hillside villas?**
Yes. On a slope the retaining walls and drainage are costed before the renovation itself — sometimes a comparable budget in their own right.
