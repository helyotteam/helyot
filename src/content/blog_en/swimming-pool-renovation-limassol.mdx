A swimming pool is one of the highest-value features of any Limassol villa — and one of the most neglected. Cracked grout, faded paint, outdated filtration and ageing pumps are common on pools over ten years old. A properly renovated pool not only looks spectacular but also uses significantly less energy and requires far less maintenance.

## Signs Your Pool Needs Renovation

| Sign | Likely Cause | Urgency |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Visible cracks in shell | Settlement or freeze-thaw cycles | High |
| Constant water loss | Failed waterproofing | High |
| Faded or peeling paint | End of paint lifecycle | Medium |
| Old pump noise / weak flow | Pump nearing end of life | Medium |
| Algae problems despite treatment | Inadequate filtration capacity | Medium |
| Cold water well into May | No heating system | Low |

## What a Full Pool Renovation Includes

A comprehensive renovation typically covers these elements:

### 1. Shell Inspection and Crack Repair
Before any surface work begins, we carry out a full structural inspection using waterproof sealant injection for hairline cracks and epoxy injection for structural cracks. A leaking pool loses 2–5mm of water per day — that is 5,000–12,000 litres per month in a standard 50m² pool.

### 2. Waterproofing System
We apply a Mapei or Sika liquid membrane system in two coats, with fibreglass mesh reinforcement at all junctions — floor-to-wall and wall-to-coping. After application, the pool is flood-tested for 24 hours before any tiling begins. This system carries our **10-year waterproofing guarantee**.

### 3. Tiling or Liner
**Mosaic tiles** are the most popular choice in Limassol villas. Glass mosaic reflects light beautifully, is chemically resistant, and has a lifespan of 25+ years. Ceramic mosaic is a more economical option with excellent durability.

**Liners** (PVC membranes) offer a fast, cost-effective renovation — typically installed in 3–5 days. They suit budget-conscious renovations but have a shorter lifespan (8–12 years).

### 4. Filtration and Pump Upgrade
Modern variable-speed pumps reduce energy consumption by 50–70% compared to single-speed units. Combined with a sand or glass-bead filter, a new system maintains cleaner water with less chemical use. We typically recommend Pentair or Hayward systems for Limassol conditions.

### 5. Pool Heating
A heat pump extending the swimming season from March to December costs €4,500–€8,000 installed. At current electricity tariffs, the running cost is approximately €80–€150 per month during shoulder months — far less than solar heating in high-use periods.

## Timeline

A standard pool renovation (mosaic re-tile + filtration upgrade) takes **3–5 weeks**:

- **Days 1–3:** Drain, inspect, crack repair
- **Days 4–7:** Waterproofing application + cure
- **Day 8:** Flood test
- **Days 9–18:** Tile installation
- **Days 19–21:** Grouting, coping, surround works
- **Days 22–25:** Equipment installation + refill
- **Days 26–28:** Water balancing, handover

## Pool Surround Works

The area immediately around the pool has an enormous impact on the overall appearance. Popular options in Limassol:

- **Porcelain slabs** (60×60 or 80×80) — low maintenance, heat-resistant
- **Natural stone** (limestone or travertine) — classic Mediterranean look but requires annual sealing
- **Composite decking** — warm underfoot, splinter-free, popular for families

We typically quote pool surround works separately from the pool itself, as clients often want to phase the investment.

## Frequently asked questions

**What does pool renovation cost?**
€8,000–25,000 depending on size and whether the shell, plant and finishes all need replacing.

**How long does it take?**
Four to eight weeks. Re-tiling and plant replacement are quick; structural repair to the shell is not.

**When should it be done?**
Autumn or winter. It keeps the pool available through the season and lets materials cure without extreme heat.

**What fails first in Cyprus pools?**
Tile beds and coping joints, from thermal movement and salt. Plant equipment is second.
