The first thing anyone considering building in Cyprus hears is "from €1,700 a metre". The first thing they see in a quote two months later is half as much again. The gap is not dishonesty — the two figures describe different things.

Here is what sits inside each price, what every stage costs, and where budgets actually go up.

## In short

A quality turnkey house in Cyprus in 2026 costs **€2,000–3,200 per m²**. The lower end is a simple house on a flat plot with a plain but honest specification. The upper end is a villa with complex geometry, premium materials and full building services.

**€1,700 per m²** buys a shell: structure, roof, windows, first-fix services. You cannot live in it — there are no finishes, no sanitary ware, no kitchen, no lighting.

A €1,200 spread per metre is €240,000 on a 200 m² house. Which is why it matters what exactly you are being quoted for.

## Where the money goes

Below is the split for a 200 m² house at a mid specification. The percentages hold; the absolute figures move.

| Stage | Share | On 200 m² at €2,400/m² |
|---|---|---|
| Site preparation and earthworks | 5–8% | €24,000–38,000 |
| Foundation | 8–12% | €38,000–58,000 |
| Structure: frame, slabs, roof | 22–26% | €106,000–125,000 |
| Windows and external doors | 7–10% | €34,000–48,000 |
| Services: electrical, plumbing, climate | 14–18% | €67,000–86,000 |
| External render and insulation | 8–11% | €38,000–53,000 |
| Internal finishes | 18–24% | €86,000–115,000 |
| Design, approvals, management | 6–9% | €29,000–43,000 |

Note that finishes and services together are a third of the budget. They are exactly what gets cut to reach "€1,700", and exactly what determines what the house is like to live in.

## Why it cannot be cheaper in 2026

From 2024–2026 all new houses must meet the **NZEB** standard — Near-Zero Energy Buildings. This is not a recommendation; it is a condition of getting your permit.

What the standard adds:

- **Wall insulation of 80–100 mm** instead of the old 30–50 mm: €40–70 per m² of wall
- **Solar panels of 4.17–6 kW**: €4,000–8,000 per house
- **Thermal windows** with low heat transfer: 25–40% more than standard units
- **Energy performance calculation** in the design: €800–1,500

In total NZEB adds 8–14% to the build cost. It comes back as lower electricity bills — the full calculation is in the [guide to energy class A+](/en/blog/energy-class-a-plus-cyprus).

## What pushes the price up

**Slope.** The most underestimated factor. Retaining walls on a slope cost €15,000–80,000 depending on the change in level — an entire stage that does not exist on a flat plot. Covered in [the piece on retaining walls](/en/blog/retaining-walls-cyprus).

**Rock.** Excavating rock instead of soil adds €10,000–20,000 in drilling. The only way to know in advance is a ground survey — before you buy the plot, not after.

**Complex geometry.** Every projection, cantilever and floor-to-ceiling glazed opening is a bespoke detail. A simple rectangular volume of the same area is 12–18% cheaper.

**Double-height space.** The volume you heat and cool grows while the usable floor area does not. It looks superb and it is priced by the cubic metre of services, not the square metre of floor.

## What the price usually excludes

Even an honest turnkey quote in Cyprus normally leaves out:

- **Furniture and appliances** — beyond a fitted kitchen, if that is specified
- **Landscaping** — from €15,000 on a 500 m² plot
- **Swimming pool** — €35,000–70,000 depending on size and finish
- **Fencing and gates** — €8,000–20,000
- **Municipal fees** — paid at cost with the receipt attached

Ask about these separately. If the contractor says "we'll sort that out later", that is a future argument.

## Timescales, and how they affect price

The full cycle is 18–28 months from buying the plot to moving in:

- Design and approvals: 3–12 months. [Real permit timelines](/en/blog/building-permit-cyprus-timeline) differ sharply from the published ones
- Construction: 10–16 months
- Finishes and handover: 2–4 months

Compressing the programme almost always costs more: night shifts, expedited deliveries, trades working in parallel rather than in sequence. A realistic schedule is cheaper than paying for haste.

## The contingency you need

**25–30% on top of the quote.** Not because the contractor will get it wrong, but because:

- the ground survey can find more than expected;
- you will change your mind about finishes mid-way — almost everyone does;
- material prices move over 18 months of building.

A budget with no contingency is not a saving, it is a deferred problem. See also [what the taxes and mandatory payments come to](/en/blog/construction-taxes-cyprus).

## How to check the quote you were given

1. **Is there a stage-by-stage breakdown**, or only a total? One number is not a quote.
2. **Is the material grade specified** for finishes, rather than just "tiles" and "paint"?
3. **What does it say about provisional sums?** "To be confirmed on site" means the price is not fixed.
4. **Is NZEB included** — insulation, panels, windows? Without it there is no permit.
5. **Is there a handover date in the contract**, rather than "approximately"?

## Frequently asked questions

**Why such a wide range — €2,000 against €3,200?**
They are different houses. The low end is a simple form on a flat plot with a solid basic specification. The high end is complex geometry, panoramic glazing, premium materials and full automation.

**Can you build for €1,700 per m²?**
You can build a shell. Living in it needs another €400–700 per metre: finishes, sanitary ware, kitchen, lighting.

**What size of house makes sense?**
Running costs in Cyprus scale almost linearly with area — cooling, cleaning, maintenance. 180–250 m² covers a family's needs without the overhead.

**Is building cheaper than buying finished?**
By 25–42% at comparable quality — [the detailed comparison with figures](/en/blog/build-or-buy-house-cyprus-2026).
