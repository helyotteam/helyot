Construction taxes in Cyprus get confused mostly because there are two separate sets: one on the land, one on the work. They are regularly mixed up, including by advisers. Here they are in order, with the numbers.

## In short

Buying a plot means paying **either** Transfer Fees (3–8%, with a 50% discount, if the seller is a private individual) **or** 19% VAT (if the seller is a company). They are mutually exclusive.

Building work carries its own VAT: **5% on the first 130 m²** for a first home (up to €350,000) and 19% on the rest.

Stamp duty was abolished on 1 January 2026. An annual sewerage board fee applies at 0.05–0.25% of the assessed value.

## Payment one: on the land

### Option A — private seller: Transfer Fees

A progressive scale, with a 50% discount available to all buyers.

| Plot value | Rate |
|---|---|
| Up to €85,000 | 3% |
| €85,001 – €170,000 | 5% |
| Above €170,000 | 8% |

**On a €250,000 plot:**

- €85,000 × 3% = €2,550
- €85,000 × 5% = €4,250
- €80,000 × 8% = €6,400
- €13,200 in total, **€6,600 after the 50% discount**

### Option B — company seller: 19% VAT

If a developer or company sells the plot, 19% VAT is already in the price and Transfer Fees are **zero**.

On the same €250,000 plot that is €47,500 of VAT — usually included in the advertised price rather than added on top. Check the wording in the contract: "price includes VAT" versus "plus VAT" is a 19% difference.

## Payment two: on the building work

Separate VAT applies to the construction contract, regardless of how you bought the land.

| Condition | Rate |
|---|---|
| First 130 m² of a first home (up to €350,000) | 5% |
| Remaining area | 19% |
| Second and subsequent properties | 19% throughout |

**On a 200 m² house costing €480,000 (first home):**

- Share of the first 130 m²: 130 / 200 = 65% → €312,000 at 5% = €15,600
- The other 70 m²: €168,000 at 19% = €31,920
- **Total VAT: €47,520**

At a flat 19% it would be €91,200. The relief is worth **€43,680**.

## Stamp duty abolished

Law 19/1963 was repealed by Law No. 239(I)/2025. No stamp duty is payable on contracts signed from 1 January 2026.

It used to be around €800–1,000 on a €450,000 house. A small sum, but one line fewer in the budget.

## Annual charges

**Sewerage board fee.** Calculated on the assessed value, not the market value, at 0.05–0.25% depending on the municipality. For a house assessed at €300,000 that is €150–400 a year.

**Municipal property tax.** €50–200 a year depending on size and area.

The national Immovable Property Tax was abolished in 2017 — if someone is charging it, that is a mistake.

## Paid at cost on top

- **Building permit fees** — dependent on area and municipality, typically €1,500–4,000
- **Title registration** at the Land Registry
- **Legal fees** on the transaction — 0.5–1% of the value

A straight contractor passes these on at cost with the receipt rather than folding them into their margin.

## Summary

| What | When | How much |
|---|---|---|
| Transfer Fees or VAT on land | On buying the plot | 3–8% with 50% discount, or 19% |
| VAT on the contract | By construction stage | 5% on first 130 m², then 19% |
| Stamp duty | — | Abolished 01/01/2026 |
| Permit fee | On submission | €1,500–4,000 |
| Sewerage board fee | Annually | 0.05–0.25% of assessed value |

## Frequently asked questions

**If the plot carries VAT, do I also pay Transfer Fees?**
No. They are mutually exclusive. A plot sold with VAT means zero Transfer Fees.

**Does the 5% relief only apply to a first home?**
Yes, and only to the first 130 m² where the value is under €350,000. A second property is taxed at 19% throughout.

**Can VAT be reclaimed?**
Not by a private individual building for their own use. Reclaim is possible for commercial use with VAT registration.

**Are taxes included in the contractor's quote?**
VAT on the contract, yes — it is inside the price of the work. Land taxes and permit fees are not; those you pay directly.
