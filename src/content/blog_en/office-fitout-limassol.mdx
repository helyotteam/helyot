Limassol's commercial real estate market has expanded significantly since 2020, driven by the relocation of technology firms, financial services companies and professional service providers from across Europe and beyond. The result: a strong demand for quality office fit-out that meets international standards — in a market where few contractors have consistent experience delivering to that level.

## The Limassol Office Market Context

Companies moving to Limassol typically lease shell-and-core offices in the newer commercial buildings — particularly in the city centre, Limassol Marina and the Germasogeia industrial zone. Shell-and-core means four walls, a concrete floor, basic plumbing connections and electrical supply — everything else is the tenant's responsibility.

The fit-out itself is where the quality of your working environment is determined. A well-designed office improves productivity, reflects your brand, helps with talent retention and sets expectations for clients and visitors.

## Office Fit-Out Scope: What It Includes

A full Category B office fit-out (the standard for occupiable office space) covers:

**Space planning and 3D design**
Floor plan layout optimised for your headcount and workflow. We present multiple options in 3D before any works begin — showing desk positions, meeting rooms, breakout areas and circulation.

**Raised access flooring (optional)**
Essential for technology and financial companies needing below-floor cable management. Adds €15–€22/m² but dramatically simplifies future changes.

**Suspended acoustic ceiling**
An acoustic grid ceiling with LED luminaires significantly reduces noise in open-plan offices and gives a clean, contemporary appearance. The space between the ceiling and slab accommodates HVAC, cable trays and data conduits.

**Glass partition systems**
Demountable glass partitions for meeting rooms and private offices. Aluminium-framed systems are most common; frameless glass is premium. Demountable systems are advantageous for tenants as they can be removed and relocated without structural changes.

**Electrical distribution**
New distribution board (consumer unit), power circuits to workstations (typically 6–8 circuits per zone), lighting circuits, and emergency lighting. All work is carried out by our CERA-certified electricians with full certification at handover.

**Data, AV and security**
Structured cabling to every desk position (Cat6A), AV systems for conference rooms, CCTV, access control and WiFi access point infrastructure. We coordinate with your IT team or supplier.

**Kitchen and breakout**
A well-designed staff kitchen is one of the most-used spaces in any office. We install cabinetry, plumbing, extraction and equipment fittings as part of the standard scope.

## Permits for Commercial Fit-Out

Commercial fit-outs in Limassol require:

1. **Municipality planning approval** for partitioning, electrical work and any structural changes
2. **Fire safety approval** from the Department of Labour (for occupancy classification)
3. **Building authority notification** for buildings under warranty

We manage all permit applications as part of our project management. Most straightforward applications are approved within 4–6 weeks.

## Working Around Your Operations

Many companies need to fit out an office while still operating from an existing space with a hard move-out deadline. Our approach:

- **Phased programme** — works planned around your lease start date, counting back from delivery
- **Out-of-hours critical works** — noisy work (breaking, drilling) scheduled for evenings and weekends where needed
- **Technology readiness** — we coordinate with your IT team to ensure data, phones and AV are live on Day 1
- **Snagging resolution** — any items identified during walkthrough are resolved before keys are handed over
