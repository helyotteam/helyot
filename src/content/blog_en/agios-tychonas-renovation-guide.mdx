Agios Tychonas is the most sought-after address in the Limassol district — and arguably one of the most prestigious residential areas in Cyprus. Perched on the hillside above the coast, it offers panoramic sea views, a cooler microclimate and a level of privacy that simply does not exist closer to the city centre. Properties here command prices of €500,000 to €3M+ and tend to be villas and large apartments built during the 1990s–2010s, many of which are now due for comprehensive renovation.

## What Makes Agios Tychonas Different

Renovating in Agios Tychonas comes with specific considerations that do not apply everywhere in Limassol:

**Steep terrain.** Most villas are built on inclined plots with retaining walls, multiple levels, and sometimes complex structural configurations. Any structural work — wall removal, extension, terrace creation — requires experienced contractors who understand hillside construction.

**Sea-facing exposure.** Properties with unobstructed sea views face prevailing south-westerly winds and significantly more salt air than city properties. This accelerates corrosion on metalwork and accelerates paint degradation on facades. Material specification matters here.

**Premium expectations.** Agios Tychonas buyers and tenants expect premium finishes. Natural stone, smart home integration, landscaped terraces and pool renovations are the norm rather than the exception.

## Common Renovation Scopes in the Area

### Full Villa Renovation
Properties from the 1990s and early 2000s typically need complete MEP (mechanical, electrical, plumbing) replacement, new flooring, kitchen and bathroom renovation, and significant facade works. A 250–350m² villa typically costs €90,000–€180,000 all-in.

### Pre-Sale Renovation
Sellers who invest €25,000–€50,000 in a targeted renovation (kitchen, bathrooms, fresh decoration, pool) consistently achieve returns of 15–35% above unrenovated comparables. We have completed several such projects in the area.

### Holiday Let Upgrade
Agios Tychonas has a strong premium short-let market. Investors upgrading villas to a 5-star standard typically see occupancy rates increase by 20–30% and nightly rates increase by 35–60% post-renovation.

## Permits in Agios Tychonas

The Agios Athanasios Municipality (which covers Agios Tychonas) processes structural permit applications. Key points:

- **Planning permission required** for any structural changes, extensions, new pool construction, or significant external alterations
- **Processing time:** typically 6–12 weeks for straightforward applications
- **Plot coverage ratios:** most zones allow 35–50% plot coverage — check your specific zone before planning an extension
- **Heritage or special zones:** some hillside areas have specific visual guidelines — we advise at the survey stage

We manage the full permit application as part of our project management. The engineering drawings, submission, Municipal liaison and permit collection are all included.

## Choosing the Right Contractor for Agios Tychonas

Given the premium property values, choosing a contractor who corners on materials or uses unregistered subcontractors is a significant risk. What to verify before signing:

1. **Licensed contractor registration** with the Cyprus Department of Labour
2. **CERA-certified electricians** — mandatory for all electrical work
3. **Professional indemnity insurance** — ask to see the certificate
4. **Fixed-price contract** — any contractor offering to work on time-and-materials is passing project management risk to you
5. **References from similar-value projects** — ask for contact details, not just photos

## Frequently asked questions

**What does a renovation cost in Agios Tychonas?**
A two-bedroom apartment runs €25,000–30,000 at economy specification and €51,000–77,000 at premium. Villas of 250 m² fall between €70,000 and €225,000 depending on level.

**Are hillside villas more expensive here?**
Yes. Most of the villa stock sits on sloping ground, which adds retaining walls, drainage and terracing — a budget line flat plots never carry.

**How long does a full renovation take?**
Six to ten weeks for an apartment, sixteen to twenty-four for a villa. Layout changes add consent time on top.

**Do management companies restrict work?**
In apartment blocks, usually yes: working hours, lift access and consent for anything on the facade. Check the rules before the quote.
