## Project Overview

This 4-bedroom hillside villa commanded exceptional views over Limassol Bay but had tired interiors from the early 2000s. The clients wanted a contemporary Mediterranean transformation.

## Scope

Full interior strip-back and rebuild: natural Jerusalem stone flooring throughout ground floor, custom-designed kitchen with island and quartzite worktop, master bathroom with freestanding bath and walk-in shower, new pool mosaic and decking, and a comprehensive smart home system.

## Result

Valued 32% higher than comparable unrenovated properties in the area.
