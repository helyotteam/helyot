## Project Overview

This 2-bedroom apartment had not been touched since 2006. The owner wanted a fresh renovation to maximise short-term rental income in Germasogeia's competitive market.

## Scope of Works

The renovation covered the full apartment: electrical rewire, new plumbing throughout, full bathroom renovation with 60×120 cm porcelain, a new kitchen layout with peninsula island, SPC flooring across all rooms, and fresh paint throughout.

## Result

The apartment now commands €1,400/month in long-term rental — up from €850 pre-renovation.
